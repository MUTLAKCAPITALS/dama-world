# Souq Al-Mubarakiya — hero building references (Phase 1)

Everything in this folder is modelled from scratch with bpy. No photoscans, no downloaded models,
no downloaded textures: every material is a procedural node tree. References below were only
*read* to get proportions and construction right; nothing from them is copied into the assets.

## Sources (reading only, no assets taken)

| Source | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| User's own photo of a Mubarakiya alley (sent in chat) | Studded dark doors, beige plaster, timber pergola with diagonal braces and hanging lanterns, grey/beige pavers with circular medallions, planters, benches |
| User's second image (stylised carved arch) | Mood only — carved panels are **not** used on the hero building because no Kuwaiti source supports them on a souq shop |
| Al-Jarida, «بو خوخة» article — aljarida.com/articles/1490256745170130100 | Door construction: teak planks ~4 cm, studs fixing planks, back battens (شلامين), central rail (الخشم), wicket ~50×130 cm in one leaf, stud rosettes (وردة/ثريا) of five rows, studs painted black, brass handles and knockers, oiled finish |
| Wikipedia, *Architecture of Kuwait* | Sea stone / rubble walls under thick mud plaster, mangrove (چندل) roof poles from East Africa, decoration concentrated on doors and windows, plain exteriors |
| Cardiff Met / ASCE, *Design philosophy of the traditional Kuwaiti house* (abstract) | Inward-looking plain facades, small high openings |
| NCCAL Mubarakiya shop restoration (Arab Times 2021, via PressReader) | Restored shop rows keep small single-room shops with wooden shutters on a raised step |

## Elements adopted (each one has a source above)

| Element | Real-world size used | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wall | 55 cm ground floor, 45 cm upper, lime/mud plaster over sea stone | Plaster lost at the base and edges shows the stone underneath |
| Plinth (wet/dirty band) | 0–60 cm | Darker, more worn |
| Main gate «بو خوخة», double leaf | 2.10 × 3.10 m opening | Vertical teak planks 4 cm, back battens, central rail, iron strap hinges, black dome studs in rows + rosettes, brass ring knocker, wicket 0.52 × 1.30 m in the right leaf |
| Timber lintel over openings | 18–22 cm deep, bearing 25 cm into the wall each side | Dark oiled hardwood |
| Shop bays | 2.3 m wide, 2.6 m high, raised step 35 cm | Folding plank shutters, interior shelves |
| Upper windows | 0.9 × 1.3 m, set deep in the wall | Iron bars + paneled wooden shutters, timber lintel |
| Roof | Mangrove poles Ø 12–14 cm at ~40 cm spacing, ends protrude 20–30 cm | Over them: reed mat + mud slab |
| Parapet | 85 cm, rounded plaster coping | Kept plain — no crenellations (no source supports them here) |
| Rain spout «مرزام» | Timber trough ~70 cm projection | One per roof drainage line |
| Pergola (modern Mubarakiya) | 15 × 15 cm posts, 20 cm beams, diagonal braces, hanging lanterns | From the user's photo |
| Pavers | 40 × 40 cm grey/beige stones, circular medallion in front of the gate | From the user's photo |

## Not used (no supporting reference)
Pointed/ogee arches, mashrabiya, crenellations, carved relief panels.
